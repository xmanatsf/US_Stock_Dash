How to read this

Two kinds of statement appear throughout, and they are styled differently on purpose.

Measurements carry a chip or a number — breadth percentages, moving-average tests, pillar scores. Each is the output of a stated rule against a stated threshold, and the rule is printed next to the result.

Interpretations — the regime label's playbook, the pillar conclusions — are plain prose with no chip and no colour coding. They follow from the measurements above them but are not themselves measured. A conclusion wearing a green chip is what makes a narrative look like a fact, so conclusions here never wear one.

Where a workbook fails validation the affected tab renders its findings instead of a chart. Where a pillar has insufficient data it is dropped and the remaining weights are renormalised, with the resulting coverage stated — never silently scored as neutral.

Static site, no backend. All computation happens in the Python pipeline under scripts/ ahead of commit; the browser fetches plain JSON. Rebuild with python scripts/build_all.py.
